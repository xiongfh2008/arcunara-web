About Xingyuan Tarot

Xingyuan Tarot is a focused tarot reading experience designed for reflection, pattern recognition, and intentional decision-making.

The reading uses the 78-card tarot deck with Single Card, Three Card, and Celtic Cross spreads. Tarot is presented as a reflective tool rather than a fixed prediction of the future.

For personal reflection and entertainment only. It is not medical, legal, financial, or other professional advice.
